# Proposal: User Management

## Goal
Manage administrative and end-user accounts, their permissions, and their personalized forest "storytelling" experience (tree history and certificates).

## Context
Users are the primary stakeholders. Admins manage the platform's operations, while end-users (adopters/sponsors) consume the forest data through personalized stories and certificates.

## Features
- **User CRUD & RBAC**: Manage user accounts with defined roles (Admin, Gestor, Operador, Visualizador) and status control.
- **Personalized Storytelling**: Create and manage chronological "stories" for purchased trees, including photos and descriptive updates.
- **Tree Data Sync**: Automatically populate user stories by syncing data from technical field updates (measurements, maintenance logs).
- **Public Visibility Control**: Toggle the public visibility of tree stories, enabling users to share their environmental impact.
- **User Certificate Repository**: Issuance and management of certificates directly linked to the user's profile.
- **QR Code Integration**: Generation of QR codes for quick access to user profiles or tree stories.

## Success Criteria
- Efficient management of system access through roles.
- High engagement through automated and manual storytelling for adopters.
- Data integrity between technical forest data and the stories presented to users.
