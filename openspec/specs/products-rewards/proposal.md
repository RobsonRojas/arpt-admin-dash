# Proposal: Products & Rewards

## Goal
Manage the commercial catalog of products and the specific reward tiers associated with forest management projects, enabling the monetization of sustainable forest assets.

## Context
Products represent the general items available (e.g., "Manejo de 1 Árvore"), while Rewards are the specific offerings linked to a Manejo project, defining price, quantity, and delivery terms.

## Features
- **Product Catalog**: Centralized management of products with multi-language support (PT/EN) and AI-assisted descriptions.
- **Reward Assignment**: Linking products to specific Manejo projects as purchasable rewards.
- **Draft Persistence**: Auto-save functionality for product and reward forms to prevent data loss.
- **Commercial Validation**: Rules for minimum name length and required fields for commercial viability.
- **Lead Time Tracking**: Management of delivery timeframes (in months) for each product/reward.
- **Active/Inactive Lifecycle**: Ability to toggle commercial availability of products and rewards.

## Success Criteria
- Commercial catalog is up-to-date and consistently translated.
- Rewards are correctly linked to projects with accurate pricing and quantities.
- Administrators can easily draft and refine product descriptions using AI.
