# Proposal: Forest Intelligence (MUSA)

## Goal
Provide strategic data visualization and AI-driven predictive insights into forest growth, species distribution, and economic valuation.

## Context
Data-driven forestry requires moving beyond raw counts to strategic understanding. MUSA (Forest Intelligence) transforms inventory data into actionable business insights using AI and advanced analytics.

## Features
- **Predictive Yield Forecasting**: Comparison between planned and realized growth, with future volume projections.
- **Ecological Mapping**: Visual distribution of species diversity within managed areas.
- **Economic Asset Valuation**: Real-time estimation of the biological asset value based on market trends and growth rates.
- **Strategic AI Insights**: Automated, context-aware recommendations generated by Gemini to guide forestry decisions.
- **Multi-dimensional Visibility**: Integrated dashboards aggregating technical, environmental, and financial forest metrics.

## Success Criteria
- Accurate visualization of growth trends over multi-year horizons.
- Actionable strategic insights generated for each key forest metric.
- Clear economic valuation reflecting the current state of biological assets.
