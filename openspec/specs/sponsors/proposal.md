# Proposal: Sponsors (RWA)

## Goal
Manage Real-World Asset (RWA) sponsors and capture new sponsorship deals, rewarding contributors who support reforestation and forest management projects.

## Context
Sponsors are key partners who provide the necessary capital for forest projects. They are categorized by levels (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) based on their total contribution.

## Features
- **Sponsor Tracking**: Maintain a directory of sponsors, categorized by type (Company, Individual).
- **Contribution Tiers**: Automatic classification of sponsors into levels based on sponsorship volume.
- **Deal Capturing**: Form for recording new sponsorship acquisition (Nova Captação).
- **Draft Recovery**: Integrated form persistence to ensure complex sponsorship data is not lost during entry.
- **Detailed Profiles**: Side drawer for viewing historical contribution data and sponsor details.

## Success Criteria
- Reliable tracking of sponsorship volumes per partner.
- Easy categorization of contributors for specialized reporting.
- Seamless deal registration workflow with data protection.
