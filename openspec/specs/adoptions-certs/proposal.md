# Proposal: Adoptions & Certificates

## Goal
Manage the tree adoption lifecycle and the issuance of official recognition certificates to sponsors and individual adopters.

## Context
Adoptions allow individuals to "own" or support specific trees. Certificates provide formal proof of this support, often linked to blockchain records for transparency.

## Features
- **Adoption Dashboard**: Overview of active adoptions, total revenue, and list of interested potential adopters.
- **Inventory Release Control**: Ability to toggle which trees from the inventory are available for public adoption.
- **Certificate Issuance**: Multi-tier certificate management (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Platinum).
- **Multi-language Certification**: Generation of certificates in both Portuguese and English.
- **Blockchain Connectivity**: Linking certificates to immutable blockchain records for verification.
- **Public View Generation**: Creation of encoded public links for adopters to share their certificates online.

## Success Criteria
- Accurate tracking of adoption status and revenue metrics.
- Efficient management of "adoptable" inventory status.
- Successful issuance and verification of certificates across all tiers and languages.
